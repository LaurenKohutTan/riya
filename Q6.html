Slacking Off

Your grades begin to drop and your parents - and you- aren't very happy. What are you going to do?

Fix Habits

You realize the consequences of your actions and vow to fix your bad habits. You study more and pay more attention in school.

Ignore

You ignore your falling grades and continue to live life. Things will work themselves out eventually.

Drop out

You decide high school isn't worth all of the stress. You drop out and seek interest elsewhere.
