Small Buisness

Your small buisness is growing slowly but steadily. However, it is also taking up a lot of your time. What are you going to do?

Drop Out

You think pursuing your buisness is your calling. School is just a distraction from your goals.

Stop

You realize the buisness is taking too much of your precious time. You would rather focus on school and try to excel academically.

Continue

You really want to make your dreams come true. You decide to continue high school and your buisness in the hopes that you can succeed despite the adversity.
