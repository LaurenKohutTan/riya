Burnout

You burn yourself out from all of the studying and overworking. What do you do?

Keep Pushing

You don't care. You keep working and don't listen to your body.

Recover

You decide to chill out on the studying a bit. You still pay attention and study consistently but ask for help when needed and don't overwork yourself.

Take A Break

You're so burnt out you realize you need a real break. You neglect school and skip many days under the alibi of being sick.
