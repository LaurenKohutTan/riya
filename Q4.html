Sports

You're now paying a sport you like. What are you going to do?

Push Yourself

You constantly push yourself past your own boundaries in order to achieve your goals. It might not be the healthiest option, but you really want to excel.

Train Consistently

You push yourself consistently but are mindful of your boundaries. You train hard for the sport but are careful to not overwork yourself.

Quit

You realize you don't really want to pursue sports, or that sports are a commitment you aren't willing to make.
