Final exams

Your diligent work has paid off and you've done super well in high school. As your final important exams come around, you realize how difficult studying is going to be. What do you do?

Push yourself

You work day in and day out, tiring yourself completely out. You feel your stress levels and general wellbeing going down the drain, but you focus on those final exams.

Study Consistently

You understand not to push yourself too much and study consistently and healthily.

Relax

You are burdened by the stress and realize you should take some time off. You chill out, hang out with friends, and take a break from your studies completely.
